🗑️ How to Delete Your Data

RelayMate uses a Zero-Server Architecture. We do not maintain any cloud databases, user accounts, or remote storage containing your messages or notification history. Because no data is stored on remote developer servers, you have full sovereign control over your data right on your device.

Option 1: Clear Local Message History

1
Open RelayMate on your Android phone.
2
Navigate to the Message Log tab.
3
Tap the "Clear Log" button in the top-right toolbar. All 200 stored local entries are immediately and irreversibly deleted from device memory.

Option 2: Complete Data Purge (Reset App)

1
Open Android Settings → Apps → RelayMate.
2
Tap Storage & Cache → Clear Storage (or uninstall the app).
3
All encryption keys, preferences, paired device tokens, and temporary files are permanently wiped from your phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did messages stop forwarding after a few hours?

Aggressive manufacturer battery optimizations (MIUI/HyperOS, Samsung OneUI, ColorOS) often put background services to sleep. In Android App Info → Battery, set RelayMate to "Unrestricted", and enable "Autostart" if available on your device.

Are my OTP codes sent in plain text?

No. When you pair using the in-app QR code, an AES-256-GCM symmetric key is generated. Every message packet is encrypted before leaving the device and decrypted only on your authorized companion phone.

How do I view OTPs on my Mac or PC without installing an app?

In RelayMate Receiver mode, enable "Desktop Web Companion". On your computer's browser connected to the same Wi-Fi, open http://relaymate.local:8080 (or your configured port). Enter your 4-digit PIN to view incoming OTPs with native desktop push alerts and sound.

Does RelayMate support smartwatches (Wear OS / Pixel Watch / Galaxy Watch)?

Yes! RelayMate includes native Wear OS enhancements featuring distinctive haptic buzz alerts for OTPs, synced notification dismissals, and a direct 1-tap [📋 Copy OTP] action right on your wrist.

How does the Channel Routing Matrix protect private codes?

Under Channel Routing Matrix, you can designate specific channels for each message category. For example, configure sensitive OTPs to forward strictly to your encrypted companion phone, while routing general notifications to a team Discord or Slack server.
